This is the first post on my personal website.

I built this place because I wanted a small corner of the internet that belongs to me: a place for project logs, programming notes, university life, and random thoughts that deserve more space than a message box.

For now, this site is simple. A homepage, a blog, a projects page, and a small "now" page.

But maybe that is enough for a beginning.

A website does not need to be perfect on day one. It just needs to exist, quietly waiting for the next sentence.
